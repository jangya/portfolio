It is a Sanskrit word: **यज्ञ (Yagya)**. One of the oldest words in the Vedic tradition.

In Odia, my mother tongue, the **ya** softens into **ja** and the **N** quietly disappears. So **yagya** travelled through centuries and a regional dialect and landed as **Jangya**.

The meaning is an offering: the act of giving something wholeheartedly, without expecting anything back. I've always liked carrying that.

— Jangya
